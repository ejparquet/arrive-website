Getting Started

Download Arrive from the App Store or Google Play. Tap "Sign Up," enter your name, email, and phone number, verify your number with the SMS code, and you're ready to browse professionals.

Yes — creating an account and browsing professionals is completely free. A small service fee may apply when you book, and this is always shown clearly before you confirm.

Bookings & Appointments

Search or browse for a professional, select a service, choose your preferred date and time from their live availability, then confirm and pay. You'll receive an instant confirmation notification.

Go to Appointments in the app, select the booking, and tap "Cancel" or "Reschedule." Cancellation policies vary by professional and are shown before booking. Always check the policy to understand any fees.

Yes. Arrive sends push notifications 24 hours and 1 hour before your appointment. Make sure notifications are enabled in your phone settings so you don't miss them.

Payments & Billing

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover), Apple Pay, and Google Pay. All payments are encrypted and processed securely.

Refunds are governed by each professional's cancellation policy. If you're eligible, refunds are processed to your original payment method within 5–10 business days. For disputes, contact our support team.

Account & Profile

Tap the profile icon in the bottom navigation, then select "Edit Profile." You can update your name, photo, phone number, and notification preferences at any time.

Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, booking history, and personal data. This action cannot be undone. Any pending bookings should be cancelled first.

On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password?" We'll send a reset link to your registered email. The link expires in 30 minutes. If you don't see it, check your spam folder.

For Professionals

Sign up with a Pro account, complete your profile with your services, pricing, and availability, then submit for review. Most profiles go live within 24 hours. It's completely free to join.

Earnings are deposited weekly to your linked bank account every Monday for the prior week's completed bookings. You can view your payout history and upcoming deposits in your Pro dashboard.

Arrive's built-in marketing suite lets you create promotions, send text and email campaigns to your client list, generate shareable social media graphics, and run loyalty reward programs — all from within the app.

Current commission rates are displayed in your Pro dashboard and are deducted automatically from each booking. We believe in transparent, fair pricing — there are no hidden fees or monthly subscriptions.
