Overview

Arrive ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the Arrive mobile application and website (collectively, the "Platform"). Please read this policy carefully. If you disagree with its terms, please discontinue use of the Platform.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account registration details (name, email address, phone number, password)
  • Profile information (profile photo, location, service preferences)
  • Payment information (processed securely through our payment partners — we do not store full card numbers)
  • Communications sent through the Platform (messages to professionals)
  • Reviews and ratings you submit

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device identifiers (device type, operating system, unique device ID)
  • Usage data (features accessed, appointments booked, session duration)
  • Location data (when you grant permission, to show nearby professionals)
  • Log data (IP address, browser type, pages visited, timestamps)
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies

Information from Third Parties

  • Social login providers (if you sign in with Apple or Google)
  • Payment processors (transaction status and confirmation)
  • Analytics partners (aggregated usage statistics)

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Facilitate bookings between clients and professionals
  • Process payments and send receipts
  • Send appointment reminders and platform notifications
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve and personalize the Platform experience
  • Detect and prevent fraud or unauthorized activity
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Send marketing communications (with your consent, and always opt-out-able)

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with:

  • Professionals on Arrive — limited profile information necessary to complete a booking
  • Service providers — payment processors, cloud hosting, analytics, and email delivery partners who are contractually bound to protect your data
  • Legal authorities — when required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of Arrive and its users
  • Business transfers — in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with notice provided to you

Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to authenticate sessions, remember preferences, and analyze usage patterns. You can control cookie behavior through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect Platform functionality.

We use analytics services including anonymized crash reporting to improve app stability. These tools collect usage data in aggregate and are not used to identify you personally.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time via the app settings or by contacting us. Some information may be retained for legal compliance, fraud prevention, or legitimate business purposes for up to 7 years.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time
  • Request a portable copy of your data
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@arrivepro.app.

Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit (TLS), encrypted storage, access controls, and regular security audits. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password and enable two-factor authentication where available.

Children's Privacy

The Arrive Platform is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please reach out:

  • Email: privacy@arrivepro.app
  • Support: Visit our Help Center

We will respond to all privacy-related inquiries within 30 days.
